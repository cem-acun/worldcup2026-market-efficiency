Upcoming matches — model vs market

Market probabilities are de-vigged (over-round removed) and averaged across all bookmakers in the latest snapshot. Model probabilities are a transparent client-side Elo estimate computed from elo_ratings.json on neutral ground (see "How the model column is computed" below). Edge = model − market; a positive edge means the model rates that outcome higher than the market does.

How the model column is computed

For each match we take the two teams' Elo ratings (R) and the expected score Ehome = 1 / (1 + 10(Raway−Rhome)/400), with no home advantage (World Cup group matches are effectively neutral). A draw mass pdraw = 0.30 · (1 − |2·Ehome − 1|) is removed, and the remainder is split between the two sides in proportion to the expected score. This is a deliberately simple, inspectable live estimate; the headline tournament probabilities on the other tabs come from the full 10,000-run Monte Carlo model in the notebooks.
